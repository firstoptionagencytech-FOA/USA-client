TASK 3 — Create the Pain Points Section
Section Heading:
Great IT Support Shouldn't Feel Disorganized.
Create three visually attractive cards.
Card 1 — Scattered Customer Requests
Problem: Customer requests arrive through calls, texts, and different communication channels.
Solution: Give customers a dedicated branded place to submit and follow requests.
Card 2 — Endless Follow-Ups
Problem: Customers repeatedly ask for updates because they don't know the status of their work.
Solution: Let customers check service progress through their own portal.
Card 3 — Quotes and Payment Confusion
Problem: Quotes, approvals, and payment information become difficult to track.
Solution: Bring the supported quote, approval, and payment workflows into one connected experience.
Design: Use simple icons and short text. Avoid long paragraphs.
TASK 4 — Show the Business Benefits
Section Heading:
Don't Just Manage Support. Build a Better Customer Experience.
Create four benefit blocks:
1. Save Administrative Time
Reduce dependence on scattered messages and repetitive status updates.
2. Create a Professional Brand Experience
Give customers a mobile app and web portal displaying your business identity.
3. Make Customer Approvals Easier
Provide a clearer process for reviewing quotes, approving work, and completing payment steps.
4. Support Repeat Business
Highlight available loyalty and referral features that can help businesses encourage returning customers.
Important: Do not promise guaranteed revenue increases, customer retention, or time savings.
